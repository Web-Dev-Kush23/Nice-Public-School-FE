Our Story

A Legacy of Quality
Education in Dadri

Established in 2000, Nice Public School has been at the forefront of imparting quality education in Greater Noida's Dadri region. We are a CBSE-affiliated institution committed to nurturing every child's academic, moral, and personal growth in a safe, stimulating environment.

Our school blends tradition with innovation — experienced faculty, state-of-the-art infrastructure, and a child-centric pedagogy — ensuring every student is prepared to excel in the 21st century.

Admission Process

Our admission process is transparent, simple, and student-friendly. We welcome families who share our vision for quality education and holistic development.

1
Collect Admission Form

Visit the school office or download the admission form from our website. Forms are available throughout the session for all classes from Nursery to Class 8.

2
Submit Required Documents

Submit completed form along with birth certificate, previous class marksheet/report card, transfer certificate (if applicable), 4 passport-size photographs, and Aadhaar card copy.

3
Interaction / Entrance Assessment

For Class 1 and above, a friendly interaction session and basic aptitude assessment is conducted to understand the student's learning level and interests.

4
Seat Confirmation & Fee Payment

Upon selection, confirm the admission by paying the first-term fees. We offer flexible fee payment options including online transfer and cash payment.

5
Welcome to Nice Public School!

Receive the school uniform, books, diary, and ID card. Orientation program for new students and parents is conducted at the start of every academic session.

Important Note

Admissions are subject to seat availability. Age criteria: Nursery – 3+ years, LKG – 4+ years, UKG – 5+ years, Class 1 – 6+ years as on 31st March of the academic year. Limited seats available — apply early!
